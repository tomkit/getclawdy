Hold a key. Say the thing you'd say anyway.

Clawdy looks at your screen, answers out loud, and the claw goes to the exact spot.

You, at 11:40pm

“What did I just break?”

Clawdy reads the dialog you've been staring at and says: "Nothing's lost. Click Revert to Saved, that one." The claw is already on it.

You, in System Settings

“Which of these do I actually need on?”

Twelve toggles, zero explanation. Clawdy: "Just this one. Leave the rest off."

You, on a government website

“I have no idea what this box wants.”

Clawdy reads the whole form, not just the box. "That's your adjusted gross income. It's line 11 on last year's return."

You, six paragraphs deep

“What do they actually want from me?”

Clawdy skips the pleasantries. "The invoice, by Friday. That sentence, right there. Everything else is padding."

You, with 14 tabs open

“Find me three laptops under $900 and put it on one page.”

Big ask, so Clawdy goes and does it: reads the web, builds the comparison, and opens it on your screen. "Done. The middle one, if you want my pick."

It already knows your Mac.
